**Headline figures (share of transformer units delivered on time):**

| 2024 | 2025 | 2026 YTD |
|---|---|---|
| 63% | 87% | 90% |

- On-time delivery has improved by **27 points** since 2024.
- It has stayed at **85% or better every quarter since Q2 2025**, with **96% in Q3 2026**.
- When a unit is late, the delay is much shorter: the median went from 77 days in 2024 to 30 days in 2026.

**How it's measured:** a unit counts as on time if it was delivered no later than 8 calendar days after its initial promised date, the same grace period we use in the Power BI OTD report. Results are counted per transformer unit and exclude repairs, services and sub-assemblies. 2026 is year to date, through September 25.

Because the current tracking system doesn't have complete delivery history back to 2024, the figures were rebuilt from the historical Jobscope records Jose provided and the production tracking archive, and each was checked against the other. The detailed data is available if it's ever needed to back up the numbers.
